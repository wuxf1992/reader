Warm light tan base close to hex #d9c29b, with very low contrast variations predominantly between #cdb58e and #e4cda8 so dark brown body text is exceptionally easy to read. Texture must be visibly recognizable as kraft paper at normal screen size, not a blank solid color. Flat lighting with identical average brightness at all four edges, seamlessly tileable in both directions. No objects, no text, no letters, no logos, no borders, no page edges, no shadows, no folds, no creases, no grunge, no stains, no dramatic highlights, no gradients or vignette. Output just the paper surface, square composition.
